# Latch — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 20 September 2026_

Latch ("the app") is an Atlassian Forge app for Confluence Cloud published by
**VYRA** ("we", "us"), Stockholm, Sweden. Contact:
**vyra.branding@gmail.com**.

This policy explains what data the app processes, why, where it is stored,
and your rights. It applies to the app only; Atlassian's own privacy policy
governs Confluence itself.

## 1. What the app does

Latch lets Confluence users mark an attachment as "checked out" so colleagues
know someone is editing it, warns people when a checked-out file is
overwritten, and notifies people who asked to be told when a file is free.

## 2. Data we process

| Data | Purpose | Where it is stored | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|
| Attachment id, file name, page or blog post id, version number | Identify the locked file | Atlassian Forge storage, inside your Atlassian site's region | Until the file is checked in, the lock expires, or the file is deleted |
| Atlassian account id and display name of the person who checked a file out | Show colleagues who has the file | Same | Same as the lock |
| Optional free-text note entered at check-out | Give colleagues context | Same | Same as the lock |
| Atlassian account ids of people waiting for a file | Notify them once when the file is free | Same | Cleared when the file is checked in |
| Atlassian account ids of the uploader and lock owner, version number and time of an overwrite | Show overwrite warnings on the page | Same | 90 days, then deleted automatically |
| Site settings entered by an administrator (group names, toggles, numbers) | Configure the app | Same | Until changed or the app is uninstalled |

We do **not** process file contents, email addresses, IP addresses, or any
data outside the Confluence site the app is installed on. The app performs no
analytics or tracking.

## 3. Where data lives and who can see it

All data is stored in Atlassian Forge hosted storage and processed by
Atlassian Forge functions. The app has no servers of its own and sends no
data to any third party. Atlassian is our sub-processor for hosting; see
[Atlassian's Forge data residency documentation](https://developer.atlassian.com/platform/forge/data-residency/)
for regions.

We (the publisher) cannot access your site's data. Support is handled from
the information you choose to send us.

## 4. Legal basis

We process this data to provide the service your organisation has installed
(performance of a contract with your organisation, GDPR art. 6(1)(b)) and in
our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and reliable (art.
6(1)(f)).

## 5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data
by contacting your Confluence administrator, who can check files in and
uninstall the app, which deletes all app data. You may also contact us at
**vyra.branding@gmail.com**. EU/EEA residents may lodge a complaint with their
supervisory authority; in Sweden this is Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY).

## 6. Data deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all data the app stored for that site, in line
with Atlassian Forge storage lifecycle. Overwrite warnings expire after 90
days on their own.

## 7. Changes

We will update this page and the "Last updated" date when the policy
changes. Material changes will also be noted in the app's Marketplace
listing.
