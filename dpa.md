# Latch — Data Processing Agreement

_Last updated: 23 September 2026_

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") applies between **VYRA**, Stockholm,
Sweden ("Processor", "we") and the organisation that installs the Latch app
for Confluence Cloud ("Controller", "you"). It forms part of the
[End User Terms](terms) and applies where we process personal data on your
behalf under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR").

## 1. Subject matter and duration

We process personal data only to provide the Latch app: recording which
Confluence attachment a person has checked out, warning about overwrites, and
notifying people who asked to be told when a file is free. Processing lasts
for the duration of your subscription and ends when the app is uninstalled.

## 2. Nature and purpose

Automated processing inside the Atlassian Forge platform, on your own
Atlassian site. We do not read, export or analyse your data.

## 3. Categories of data subjects

Users of your Confluence site who check files out, wait for files, or upload
attachments.

## 4. Types of personal data

Atlassian account identifiers only. We do not store names, email addresses,
IP addresses, avatars or file contents. Display names are read from
Confluence at the moment they are shown and are never written to storage.

Free-text notes entered by a user when checking a file out are stored as
entered; users are instructed to describe the work, not to include personal
data.

## 5. Our obligations

We will:

1. Process personal data only on your documented instructions, which are
   given by your configuration and use of the app.
2. Ensure that anyone authorised to process the data is bound by
   confidentiality.
3. Implement appropriate technical and organisational measures, described in
   our [Security Policy](security). All data remains inside Atlassian Forge
   storage on your own site; we operate no infrastructure of our own and have
   no standing access to your data.
4. Assist you, to the extent we are able, with data subject requests,
   security, breach notification and impact assessments. Because we hold no
   copy of your data, most requests are satisfied by your Confluence
   administrator directly.
5. Notify you without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data
   breach affecting the app.
6. Delete the data on termination: uninstalling the app removes the app's
   storage for your site.
7. Make available the information necessary to demonstrate compliance and
   allow for audits, which we will satisfy in the first instance through this
   DPA, our Security Policy and the app's Marketplace Privacy and Security
   tab.

## 6. Sub-processors

Our only sub-processor is **Atlassian**, which hosts the Forge platform and
its storage. You already have a direct relationship with Atlassian as your
Confluence provider. We will inform you before adding any further
sub-processor and you may object.

## 7. International transfers

The app stores data in Atlassian's Forge hosted storage, which follows the
data residency of your Atlassian site. We do not transfer data outside that
location ourselves.

## 8. Liability

Liability under this DPA is subject to the limits in the End User Terms and
the Atlassian Marketplace Terms of Use.

## 9. Contact

Questions about this DPA: see the support contact on the app's Marketplace
listing.
